THE BADGER CAVE : EXECUTION OVER APPLAUSE

by Renzo Harmse
published on June 17, 2025

EXECUTION WITHOUT COMMUNICATION IS JUST NOISE

This morning at 5am, we launched the first Ground Zero session under the The Badger Cave . The theme? Execution Over Applause. But what unfolded cracked open something deeper — something I didn’t fully expect.

The power of communication, feedback, and being witnessed by a trusted community is heavily underestimated.

In that dark, quiet hour — before the noise of the world kicked in — a handful of us showed up not for hype, but for truth. We spoke openly. We listened harder. We sharpened one another. It felt less like a meeting and more like a moment of initiation — a declaration that we were done talking and ready to move.

But here's what surprised me: execution isn’t just about discipline or drive. It’s about what happens before the move. And that’s where most of us get stuck.

Too often, we get ambushed by our own self-limiting beliefs. We carry inner badgers — doubts, old stories, insecurities — that whisper, “Who do you think you are?” So we hesitate. We delay. We plan endlessly, waiting for certainty before we act.

This morning, we challenged that head-on.

We reminded each other that it’s okay to not have every answer. But that’s not a reason to freeze. Instead, it’s a signal to seek feedback — to bring your idea, your draft, your pitch, your plan — into the light of trusted perspectives.

That’s what this space gave us: permission to ask for input without losing momentum.
Clarity through conversation. Direction through feedback. Then — execution.

Especially for those of us with “Activator” in our CliftonStrengths — the starters, the initiators, the vision-driven — we often see the big picture clearly. We feel the pulse of what could be. But we forget the importance of the details — the systems, the communication, the transfer of vision into something tangible others can grasp and run with.

Because execution without clarity? That’s just chaos in motion.

Here’s what I took from today:

  • Execution matters. But so do feedback loops and systems.

  • Your inner voice isn’t always right. Some of those doubts aren’t yours — they’re inherited, outdated, or flat-out wrong.

  • Community creates courage. When others reflect your strength back to you, it quiets the badger inside.

  • Do the thing — but invite trusted feedback before you release it. Don’t aim for perfection. Aim for clarity and momentum.

That 5am call? It felt like a spark. A sacred one. Not a place for applause, but a forge where something real is being built.

We move now. With intention. With feedback. With fire.

No applause. Just action.
