REFORGED

Reforged Programme: A Journey to Discipline and Purpose

The Reforge Program wasn’t born in a boardroom or a classroom. It began as a response to the hollow leadership advice I saw around me—advice that lacked real-world grit and authenticity.The Reforged Programme took shape during a 10-day silent, plant medicine retreat in Peru, where I stripped away all distractions and focused purely on introspection and nature.

Reforged
21-Day Rite of Passage for Men and Woman Ready to Rebuild built for individuals in  their 30s and 40s who’ve faced adversity and want to rebuild silently—with discipline, purpose, and code.

THE PROBLEM

Modern adults are drowning in noise, distraction, and unprocessed pain.

  • Burnout. Disconnection. Softness.
  • Therapy is useful—but many men and woman crave action, clarity, and systems.
  • A different take on personal development. We need a reset built on code.

THE SOLUTION

Reforge Programme
A 21-day protocol that rebuilds through rhythm, systems, and alignment.

Not coaching. Not a retreat. Not therapy.
It’s a solo rebuild, backed by community, built for those who walk through fire.

AXIS 

Your inner compass. Your recalibration system. Your daily mirror.

During the Reforged programme, we don’t just chase success, we build it from the inside out. That requires daily discipline, deep self-awareness, and brutal honesty. The AXIS Tool is how we do that.

AXIS stands for the five interconnected aspects of every human's foundation:

  • 🧠 Intellectual – Are you learning, focused, mentally sharp?

  • 💪 Physical – Are you training, recovering, fueling your body like a weapon?

  • ❤️ Emotional – Are you regulated or reactive? Numb or connected?

  • 🤝 Relational – Are you showing up for your people? Or hiding in your own world?

  • 🕊️ Spiritual – Are you aligned with your values, your code, your higher power?

Every day, you’ll rate yourself 1–10 across each axis. It’s not about judgment. It’s about awareness. You can’t fix what you’re not facing.

 

Why AXIS Matters

In a world full of noise, distraction, and diluted masculinity and femininity, AXIS gives you a clear signal. It’s your internal checkpoint, a daily ritual that holds you accountable and reveals patterns before they become problems.

Most people drift.
We calibrate.

You’ll track your AXIS score throughout the 21-day Reforged experience to build rhythm, identify leverage points, and realign with your character.
